Wallet-to-wallet tracer

Follow money from one wallet onward, hop by hop. Leave the destination empty to see where the money went, or fill it in to check whether money flowed from one wallet to another.

Money is followed in one token at a time, and a hop only counts if it happened after the money arrived. A first trace can take a minute or two. Repeats are fast because wallets are saved.
